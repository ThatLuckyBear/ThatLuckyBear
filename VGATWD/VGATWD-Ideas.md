### learning from others is using a walkthrough
- game concept: Hard things are easier if someone who's done it before teaches you where the item is or how to beat the boss
- life concept: teachers, lessons, books, and tutorials are essential to getting better at skills and you severely limit your effectiveness by ignoring or avoiding them
- how they tie together: Sometimes you want to figure things out on your own, fair. Don't hamstring your potential by not learning from others.`

### Everything has a META (but you can't always access it)
- game concept: there's always a most effective way to play a game with a goal or competitive aspect
- life concept: there's always a most effective tactic to most situations (catch more bees with honey than vinegar) but they're not as obvious and cannot be mapped out as easily 
- how they tie together: Just knowing there's a better way to do something can really change the way you think about a problem or situation. Taking the time to think about what you're doing and if there's as better way can open up pathways and while you may never truly find the "most effective tactics available" you might find better ones and learn tips and tricks from others (VGATWD is a good example of this)

### New Years Resolutions are Challenge Mode Modifiers
- like Hades punishment or in hollow Knight
- not goals per sey: not necessary to complete but worthwhile challenges but sometimes they are goals it's confusing but just don't take it so literally and you'll be fine
- make things harder but have a benefit if you do complete them 
- often something people want to do but can't yet 
- usually take more than one attempt

### Small Talk is a Charm Spell
- opening with small talk isn't a waste of time it's a charm spell that sets the other at ease and communicates their intent and feelings about the other person before getting to the more challenging topic
- "I still want to be your friend. there is a problem or something I need from you."
- I like you and I want to reestablish thst before I talk to you about something more serious
- can be done poorly

### Impulsivity is a Berserk Spell
- doing or saying things without thinking happens and can be frustrating and it's hard to overcome
- you can roll to dispell but you need to learn to remember to roll before each action to see if you can do what you want

### Rejection is a Respawn not a Game Over
- when you die in a game you get to try again
- if a job/girl/friend/opportunity does not choose you can try something else
- Games let you pick up where you left off, sometimes it's a short setback (falling off a ledge in Hollow Knight) sometimes the setback is large (running out of health in Hollow Knight) but you always learn something and you get to try again. Life works the same way. If you're rejected you learn something new and try again. The only failure state in both examples is not trying.

### Gender Expression is Character Customization
- game concept: you can dress, speak, and look however you want in games like Monster Hunter/FFXIV
- life concept: you can dress, speak, and look however you want in life, often acting out a different gender in games is a first step for many to find their own preferences
- how they tie together: IN life people can customize their character, yes even gender. Just like in games this is harmless to everyone no matter how you feel about the way someone else looks.
