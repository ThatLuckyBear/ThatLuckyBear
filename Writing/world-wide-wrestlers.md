# WWW premise 

world wide wrestlers is a comic/manga idea that follows the evolution of pro wrestling through fictional wrestlers and promotions around the world. Every narrative borrows from reality and pro wrestling tropes but everything is 100% "real" in universe, no kayfabe. Magical wrestlers, foreign invaders, etc.

# ideas

- OG heel vs face where closed fist strikes are against the rules and the face has to choose between lowering themselves to the heels level or losing

- manager hates the new champion and forces them to defend the belt way more times than is necessary or normal

- group of mystery wrestlers who interrupt matches to uphold a misguided idea of what wrestlers deserve to win

- foreign invaders takes down the American hero and remains a heel even though he's justified due to the terror perpetrated by the US on his country. Slowly he takes over as the face.
